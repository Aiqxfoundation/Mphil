# University of Sargodha - MPhil Chemistry Master Syllabus Map

This document establishes the official syllabus map, topic hierarchy, learning objectives, and verification taxonomy for all seven subjects in the MPhil Chemistry Study Hub.

---

## Subject 1: Scientific Writing & Research Methods
- **Reference Code**: CHEM-7101 (User-Supplied Syllabus Reference)
- **Session/Degree**: MPhil Chemistry (Research Methodology & Scientific Communication)
- **Verification Status**: `VERIFIED IN USER-SUPPLIED SYLLABUS` (With cross-reference to HEC National Curriculum Guidelines)
- **18-Week Distribution**:
  - Week 1: Literature survey fundamentals, primary/secondary sources
  - Week 2: Chemical Abstracts, author and formula indices
  - Week 3: Systematic keyword formulation, Boolean logic
  - Week 4: Library cataloguing, central repository and electronic databases
  - Week 5: Digital search engines: SciFinder-n, Reaxys, Web of Science, PubChem
  - Week 6: Literature presentation and oral seminar techniques
  - Week 7: Citation standards (ACS, RSC, Elsevier), EndNote, Mendeley
  - Week 8: Author guidelines, manuscript structure (IMRaD), LaTeX/Word templates
  - Week 9: Midterm Examination
  - Week 10: Research synopsis preparation: title, rationale, hypotheses, methodology
  - Week 11: MPhil thesis composition: chapter organization, formatting, defense defense
  - Week 12: Research articles vs. review articles vs. conference proceedings
  - Week 13: Technical communications, patent applications, intellectual property
  - Week 14: Journal metrics: Impact Factor (Clarivate JCR), h-index, i10-index
  - Week 15: Indexing systems: Scopus, SCImago (SJR), HEC Journal Recognition System (HJRS: W, X, Y)
  - Week 16: Academic integrity, Turnitin similarity index, fabrication, falsification, retraction
  - Week 17: Research ethics: bioethics, animal handling, IRB approval, responsible AI usage
  - Week 18: Final Semester Examination

## Subject 3: Surface Chemistry
- **Reference Code**: CHEM-7124 (University of Sargodha Physical Chemistry Course)
- **Source**: UOS MPhil Chemistry Prospectus & Course Outlines
- **Verification Status**: `VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE`
- **Module Breakdown**:
  - Module 1: Fundamentals of Surfaces & Interfaces (Surface Tension, Surface Free Energy, Cohesive/Adhesive Forces, Physical Adsorption vs. Chemisorption, Thermodynamic Drivers)
  - Module 2: Adsorption Isotherms & Models (Langmuir Isotherm Derivation & Linearization, Freundlich Empirical Isotherm, BET Multilayer Adsorption Theory, Henry's Law in Dilute Systems)
  - Module 3: Gas-Solid Adsorption & Porosimetry (Specific Surface Area Determination via N2 Physisorption at 77K, IUPAC Classification of Isotherms Types I-VI, Mesopores & Micropores, BJH Pore Size Analysis)
  - Module 4: Interfacial Thermodynamics & Capillarity (Gibbs Adsorption Equation, Surface Excess Concentration Γ, Young-Laplace Equation, Contact Angle & Wetting, Young's Equation, Marangoni Effect)
  - Module 5: Surfactants, Micelles & Colloidal Stability (Surfactant Thermodynamics, Critical Micelle Concentration - CMC, Electrical Double Layer - Helmholtz & Gouy-Chapman-Stern, Zeta Potential, DLVO Theory of Colloidal Stability)
  - Module 6: Surface Characterization Techniques (Principles, Instrumentation & Interpretation of SEM, HR-TEM, AFM, STM, XPS, LEED, Field Emission Microscopy - FEM)
  - Module 7: Heterogeneous Catalysis & Interfacial Reactions (Langmuir-Hinshelwood vs. Eley-Rideal Mechanisms, Volcano Plots, Sabatier Principle, Catalyst Poisoning, Nanostructured Catalyst Design)

## Subject 4: Polymers and Advanced Composite Materials
- **Reference Code**: CHEM-7119 (University of Sargodha Chemistry Course)
- **Source**: UOS MPhil Chemistry Course Registry
- **Verification Status**: `VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE`
- **Module Breakdown**:
  - Module 1: Polymer Fundamentals & Classification (Monomers, Repeat Units, Thermoplastics vs. Thermosets, Elastomers, Engineering Plastics, Tacticity: Isotactic, Syndiotactic, Atactic)
  - Module 2: Polymerization Kinetics & Mechanisms (Step-Growth vs. Chain-Growth, Free Radical Mechanism: Initiation, Propagation, Termination, Trommsdorff-Norrish Gel Effect, Ionic & Coordination Ziegler-Natta Polymerization)
  - Module 3: Polymer Conformation, Morphology & Transitions (Amorphous vs. Semicrystalline, Glass Transition Temperature Tg and Melting Tm, Factors Influencing Tg, Free Volume Theory, Crystallinity by XRD)
  - Module 4: Molecular Weight & Polydispersity (Number-Average Mn, Weight-Average Mw, Z-Average Mz, Polydispersity Index PDI, Osmometry, Light Scattering, Viscometry Mark-Houwink-Sakurada Equation)
  - Module 5: Polymer Solutions & Thermodynamics (Flory-Huggins Lattice Theory, Chi Interaction Parameter χ, Theta Conditions, Phase Separation: Binodal and Spinodal Curves, Plasticization & Hydrogels)
  - Module 6: Comprehensive Characterization (GPC/SEC Separation, DSC Thermograms, TGA Thermal Degradation Kinetics, DMA Viscoelastic Storage/Loss Moduli, FTIR Functional Groups)
  - Module 7: Mechanical & Rheological Behavior (Stress-Strain Curves, Tensile Strength, Modulus of Elasticity, Viscoelasticity: Maxwell and Kelvin-Voigt Models, Creep and Stress Relaxation)
  - Module 8: Advanced Composite Materials (Matrix & Reinforcements, Fiber-Reinforced Polymers CFRP/GFRP, Interfacial Shear Stress & Load Transfer, Rule of Mixtures for Longitudinal/Transverse Modulus, Halpin-Tsai Models)
  - Module 9: Smart, Functional & Sustainable Polymers (Intrinsically Conducting Polymers: Polyaniline, PEDOT:PSS, Biodegradable Polymers: PLA, PHA, Self-Healing Polymers, Chemical & Mechanical Recycling)

## Subject 5: Advanced Photochemistry
- **Reference Code**: CHEM-7123 (University of Sargodha Physical Chemistry Course)
- **Source**: UOS MPhil Chemistry Course Registry
- **Verification Status**: `VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE`
- **Module Breakdown**:
  - Module 1: Radiation & Photophysical Fundamentals (Nature of Light, Photon Energy E=hν=hc/λ, Electronic Transitions σ→σ*, π→π*, n→π*, Ground vs. Singlet & Triplet Excited States, Frank-Condon Principle)
  - Module 2: Laws of Light Absorption & Spectrophotometry (Beer-Lambert Law, Molar Absorptivity ε, Deviations from Beer's Law, Auxochromes, Bathochromic & Hypsochromic Shifts)
  - Module 3: Excited State Deactivation & Jablonski Diagram (Radiative Transitions: Fluorescence & Phosphorescence, Non-Radiative Transitions: Internal Conversion - IC, Intersystem Crossing - ISC, Vibrational Relaxation, Excited State Lifetimes)
  - Module 4: Photochemical Laws & Quantum Yield (Grotthuss-Draper Law, Stark-Einstein Law of Photochemical Equivalence, Quantum Yield Φ Determination, Primary vs. Secondary Processes, Deviations & Chain Reactions)
  - Module 5: Experimental Photochemistry & Actinometry (Light Sources: High-Pressure Xe/Hg lamps, Monochromators, Chemical Actinometers: Potassium Ferrioxalate, Reinecke's Salt, Uranyl Oxalate, Photon Flux Measurement)
  - Module 6: Quenching Kinetics & Bimolecular Photoprocesses (Collisional Quenching, Stern-Volmer Equation & Plot, Dynamic vs. Static Quenching, Resonance Energy Transfer - FRET, Dexter Electron Transfer, Excimers & Exciplexes)
  - Module 7: Advanced Applications & Photocatalysis (Semiconductor Photocatalysis: TiO2 Bandgap, Electron-Hole Pair Generation, Advanced Oxidation Processes - AOPs, Solar Photovoltaics: Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells - DSSCs, Photopolymerization)

## Subject 7: Translation of Holy Quran
- **Reference Code**: HQ-7001 (HEC & Government of Punjab Postgraduate Mandatory Curriculum)

- **Verification Status**: `VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE`
- **Assigned Passages & Curriculum**:

  - Passage 2: Surah Al-Furqan (Surah 25, Ayat 63-77) - Characteristics of "Ibadur Rahman" (Servants of the Merciful), Humility, Balanced Spending, Avoiding Falsehood
  - Passage 3: Surah Al-Baqarah (Surah 2, Ayah 255 - Ayatul Kursi) - Monotheism, Divine Sovereignty, Absolute Knowledge and Eternal Attribute
  - Passage 4: Surah Al-Baqarah (Surah 2, Ayat 284-286) - Divine Accountability, Ease of Religion, Complete Supplication
  - Passage 5: Surah Luqman (Surah 31, Ayat 12-19) - Parental Devotion, Gratitude, Luqman's Counsel on Piety, Humility, and Avoiding Conceit
  - Passage 6: Surah Al-Hashr (Surah 59, Ayat 18-24) - God-Consciousness (Taqwa), Beautiful Divine Attributes (Asma-ul-Husna)
- **Pedagogical Structure**:
  - Verified Uthmani Arabic script with full vocalization (Tashkeel)
  - Word-by-word vocabulary breakdown and root word analysis
  - Standard, recognized Urdu translations (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari & Mufti Taqi Usmani)
  - Authorized English translations (Sahih International & Pickthall)
  - Distinct separation of translation vs. academic historical context vs. moral principles
  - Postgraduate examination preparation: Short Q&As, Detailed Q&As, Lexicon flashcards, Objective MCQs
